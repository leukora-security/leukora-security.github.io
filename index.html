LEUKORA Security is an AI-assisted defensive cybersecurity research initiative focused on helping organizations detect threats, contain authorized risks, and verify security outcomes.

In active R&D · Not a production-certified product
Our mission

Security that responds — and verifies.

Our vision is a digital immune system that combines security telemetry, contextual risk analysis, policy-controlled defensive actions, independent outcome verification, and governed learning. We prioritize evidence, authorization boundaries, auditability, and operational safety.

Research areas

Current engineering focus

Active Defense

Research into event correlation, policy-based containment, reversible response actions, verification, and safe recovery.

Database Protection

Monitoring suspicious access patterns and investigating data-exfiltration prevention across application and database activity.

AI Agent Security

Studying agent permission boundaries, abnormal tool use, unauthorized access attempts, and accountable response controls.

Controlled Evolution

Developing case-based analysis and carefully governed improvements with testing, review, and human approval.

Development status: These capabilities are under development and laboratory evaluation. Individual components have been tested, but the integrated system has not received final production-security approval. Performance and protection claims require independent validation.

Responsible cybersecurity research

Authorized testing only.

Our security research is designed for company-owned systems and environments where explicit permission has been granted. Adversarial testing is confined to isolated laboratories or appropriately authorized scopes. We do not endorse unauthorized intrusion, third-party disruption, or uncontrolled deployment of offensive capabilities.

Research safeguards include scoped authorization, human oversight for high-impact actions, evidence preservation, audit trails, isolated testing, independent verification, and conservative production release gates.

Why advanced cybersecurity models?

We seek to evaluate advanced AI capabilities for vulnerability analysis, authorized red-team simulation, defensive bypass assessment, security remediation, and independent validation of fixes. Access to restricted capabilities would remain subject to provider approval and applicable controls.
